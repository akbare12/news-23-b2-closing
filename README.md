One image summing up the world's news of **Monday 23 November 2026**.

**The angle — closing results, not morning wires.** The two leads are the day's
final outcomes: Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO (shares +33%), and the
Kospi plunges as much as 5.8% intraday as the AI rout spreads to Asia. Around
them: 30 stories across 6 regions, each labelled REPORTED / CLAIM / DENIAL /
ONGOING with its source, a numbered world map, an hourly UTC timeline of when
each story was reported, a "NOT YET" strip for what had not happened, and a
date-check footer naming what was excluded (COP31 collapse itself, 22 Nov;
Dems 258 tally, 22 Nov; Friday Wall St rout, 20 Nov).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png` and verifies the copy by size and sha256; both runs write
identical bytes.
